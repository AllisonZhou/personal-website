#AZ-001 / 07

School density & home prices

Published research · Cal State
Problem
Prior hedonic housing models priced in school quality (test scores, GPAs, charter status) but never the number of schools nearby. Was school density a missing variable?
Method
Built a dataset of 21,612 King County, WA home sales. Used GIS to geocode schools and engineer a new feature: the count of schools within a 10-mile radius of each home. Estimated regression and machine learning models in Python with school density added on top of every established control.
Result
School density was statistically significant over and above all existing factors. Paper accepted by the International Journal of Information and Decision Sciences; CSUPOM Conference Student Recognition Award.
StackPythonGISRegressionMachine learningFeature engineering
#AZ-002 / 07

Human bias in truck inspections

Carnegie Mellon · Research assistant
Problem
AI systems for safety-critical industrial truck inspections learn from human-labeled data, and human inspectors carry bias.
Method
Analyzed vehicle-inspection data from California and Pennsylvania with a team of CMU doctoral and undergraduate researchers, applying machine learning models to train AI on human pattern recognition and drawing on behavioral-science concepts about AI reliability in safety-related settings.
Result
Found that modeling alone was limited: accounting for human inspection behavior improved the data and the efficacy of the inspection models.
