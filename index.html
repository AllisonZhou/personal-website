A little about me

I'm a designer who codes (or a coder who designs — it depends on the day). I care about the small moments: a hover that feels alive, a transition that explains itself, type that has room to breathe.

Right now I'm exploring how generative systems and motion can make the web feel less like a document and more like a place. Before this, I worked on products used by millions and prototypes used by exactly one person (me).
